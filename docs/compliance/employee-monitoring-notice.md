# Employee Monitoring Notice (template)

> Circulate to all staff and record acknowledgement before activating monitoring.
> Replace bracketed fields and have counsel review for your jurisdiction(s).

**To:** All employees of Vision Freights LLC
**From:** [Managing Director / Data Protection Officer]
**Date:** [date]
**Subject:** Notice of monitoring of company communications systems

## What we monitor
To protect customers, company information, and operations, Vision Freights LLC
monitors and analyzes activity on **company-provided** systems, including
company email (Google Workspace), company Drive/file sharing, calendar, and
related business applications. This includes the content and metadata of
business communications sent or received using company accounts.

## Why we monitor
- Preventing leakage of confidential pricing, quotations, contracts, and
  customer data.
- Detecting security threats, unauthorized access, and data exfiltration.
- Ensuring service levels (timely responses to customer enquiries) and
  regulatory/compliance obligations.
- Investigating suspected policy breaches or unlawful activity.

**Lawful basis:** [legitimate interests of the business / compliance with legal
obligations / consent — as determined in the DPIA].

## What this means for you
- Company systems are **for business use**. You should have **no expectation of
  privacy** in communications made using company accounts.
- Keep personal matters off company systems. Clearly personal messages are
  handled with data-minimization and are not the purpose of this monitoring.
- Access to monitored content is **restricted** to authorized senior personnel
  and is **logged** (every access is auditable).

## Your rights
You may request access to personal data we hold about you, ask for correction,
and raise concerns with [DPO contact]. See the company privacy notice and the
data-subject request procedure.

## Acknowledgement
By continuing to use company systems you acknowledge this notice. Please confirm
receipt to [HR/DPO] by [date].

— [Name, title]
