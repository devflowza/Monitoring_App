# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — monitoring clause (skeleton)

Add/confirm the following in the company AUP that every employee signs. This is
the policy hook the monitoring notice and DPIA rely on.

## Company systems and monitoring
1. Email, Drive, calendar, and other systems provided by Vision Freights LLC are
   **company property, provided for business use**.
2. The company **monitors and analyzes** use of these systems for security,
   compliance, data-protection, and service-quality purposes, as described in
   the Employee Monitoring Notice.
3. Employees should have **no expectation of privacy** in communications or files
   created, sent, received, or stored on company systems.
4. Personal use should be minimal and kept off company systems where possible.
5. Prohibited: sending confidential company or customer data to personal or
   unauthorized external accounts; sharing sensitive files outside the company
   without authorization; configuring auto-forwarding of company mail to
   external addresses; bulk export of customer or pricing data.
6. Access to monitored content by authorized personnel is **restricted and
   logged**; misuse of that access is itself a disciplinary matter.

> Cross-reference this section from the Employee Monitoring Notice and the DPIA.
> Keep version + acknowledgement records.
